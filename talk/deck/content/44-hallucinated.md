- 16 models, 576,000 samples: **19.7%** of packages didn't exist
- **43%** of those invented names came back in all 10 reruns
- 2026 frontier models: 4.6–6.1%, but all five invent the same **127** names

## Aside

A name a model invents reliably is a name an attacker can register.
